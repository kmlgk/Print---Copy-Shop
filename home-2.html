OUR PROCESS

What happens after you hit submit.

STEP 01

We check your file

Bleed, resolution and colour profile are verified before anything hits the press — we flag issues instead of printing them.

STEP 02

We colour-match

Brand colours are checked against Pantone references so your cards, banners and reprints stay consistent every time.

STEP 03

We finish & trim

Binding, laminating, grommets or trimming — done in-house on the same day the job is printed.
