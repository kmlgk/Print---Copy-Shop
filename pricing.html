PRICING FAQ

A few things people ask.

Are these prices final?

Yes for standard specs. Unusual stock, oversized runs or custom die-cuts get a direct quote instead.

Is there a minimum order?

No minimum on documents or photo prints. Business cards are sold by the box (100 units).

How does Rush pricing work?

Select “Rush” when placing your order in the dashboard — it adds a flat 50% and moves your job to the front of the queue.

Do you price-match?

Send us a competitor quote for an identical spec and we’ll do our best to match it.
