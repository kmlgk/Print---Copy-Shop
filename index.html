😁
🖨️

PDF is preferred for the sharpest results. We also accept AI, PNG, JPG and DOCX — upload directly from the New Print Job page in your dashboard.

😁

Yes — every past order in your dashboard has a “Reorder” button. It reuses the exact file and specs already on file, so there’s nothing to re-upload.

Free local delivery is available within 5 miles of our Springfield shop for orders over $50. Otherwise, pickup is free anytime during business hours.
